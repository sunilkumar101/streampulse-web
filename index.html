Frequently asked questions

Does StreamPulse include channels or subscriptions?

No. StreamPulse is a media player only. You add your own legally obtained M3U playlist, Xtream Codes login or Stalker portal. A built-in demo uses Creative Commons films from the Blender Foundation.

Which playlist formats are supported?

M3U and M3U8 playlists (URL or file), Xtream Codes API logins, Stalker/Ministra portals with MAC address, and XMLTV EPG guides in .xml or .xml.gz format.

Can I watch programmes that already aired?

Yes. If your provider supports archives, StreamPulse offers 7-day catch-up TV and timeshift with full scrubbing.

Is my Xtream password safe?

Passwords are stored only in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. Cloud sync payloads are encrypted with AES-256 before upload.

Does it work on Android TV and Apple TV?

Yes. StreamPulse has a D-pad friendly TV layout and a phone-to-TV Wi-Fi QR pairing flow.

StreamPulse does not host, provide or sell any media streams or subscriptions. Users must provide their own legally sourced content.
