M3U vs Xtream Codes: which should you use?

M3U / M3U8Xtream Codes
SetupOne URL or fileServer + username + password
Movies & seriesFlat listCategories, seasons, episodes
EPGSeparate XMLTV URLBuilt in
Catch-up TVVia catchup= tagsVia tv_archive
Large listsCan be 150 MB+Paged JSON

Our recommendation

If your provider offers both, choose Xtream Codes for richer VOD and automatic EPG. Use M3U for local files, custom curated lists or providers that only publish a URL. StreamPulse parses both in a background isolate, so even 200,000-entry M3U files open without freezing.
