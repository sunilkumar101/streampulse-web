1. How StreamPulse works (Bring Your Own Playlist)

StreamPulse is a standalone video player, like VLC. It does not provide, sell or include any playlists, channels, subscriptions or media. You add playlists you already have the right to watch.

  • Add an M3U playlist from a TV service you subscribe to, from your own home media server, or from a file on your device.
  • Or sign in with the Xtream Codes details given to you by your own TV service.
  • Logins are stored only in your device's secure keychain and are never sent to us.
  • Want to try the player first? Tap Launch Instant Demo — it plays Creative Commons (CC-BY) open movies by the Blender Foundation.

2. Add an M3U / M3U8 playlist or local file

  1. Get your playlist link from your TV operator's customer portal, or from a home media server (such as Plex or Jellyfin) running on your own network.
  2. Copy the link. It usually ends in .m3u or .m3u8.
  3. Paste it into StreamPulse → M3U / M3U8 Playlist. If a link is on your clipboard, tap Paste URL from Clipboard. StreamPulse checks the link first and tells you if it is a valid playlist, a web page, or unreachable.
  4. Local files: enter the path of an .m3u file stored on your device.

A valid playlist starts with #EXTM3U; each #EXTINF line names the next address:

#EXTM3U
#EXTINF:-1 tvg-id="news.example" group-title="News",Example News HD
https://media.example.com/live/news/index.m3u8
#EXTINF:-1 tvg-id="movies.example" group-title="Movies",Home Movie Night
http://192.168.1.20:8096/videos/movie-night.mkv

3. Connect with the Xtream Codes API

Your TV service may give you three details, usually in its welcome email or customer portal:

  • Server URL — the address and port only, e.g. http://example-server:8080. No /get.php, no username in the address, no trailing /.
  • Username and Password — stored only in your device's secure keychain.

In the app, open Need help finding playlist credentials? → Xtream API and tap Test Connection & Diagnose Error. It checks the address format, http vs https and whether the server answers — without sending your username or password. Common fixes:

  • Invalid URL format / typo — copy the address again from your service.
  • Use http:// instead of https:// — many servers do not support secure connections.
  • Connection refused — the port number (after the :) is wrong.
  • This is a website, not an Xtream server — the address is incomplete; ask your service for the exact server URL.
  • Server unreachable — check your Wi-Fi, or set a custom User-Agent in Settings → Playback.

4. Phone-to-TV Wi-Fi QR transfer

  1. Connect your phone and your TV (or TV box) to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the TV: open StreamPulse → Phone Wi-Fi QR Sync → Start Companion Server. A QR code and pairing code appear.
  3. On the phone: open StreamPulse → Phone Wi-Fi QR Sync and scan the QR code on the TV.
  4. Choose M3U URL or Xtream, enter the details and tap Send to TV.

The transfer stays inside your home network and stops when you close the screen. Nothing is uploaded.

Quick fixes

  • Stream shows HTTP 403: Settings → Playback → set a custom User-Agent (for example VLC/3.0) or Referer for the playlist.
  • Buffering: Settings → Speed Test. 25 Mbps+ is recommended for 4K. Increase the buffer size to 250 MB or 512 MB.
  • Empty EPG: Settings → EPG Auto-Update → Refresh now, or paste your provider's XMLTV URL.
  • Too many offline channels: Settings → Scan & Hide Dead Channels.
